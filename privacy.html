How we treat your information.

Plain-language privacy policy for Space Between Wellness. Last updated 4 June 2026.

This is our privacy policy. It explains what information we collect when you visit this site or reach out to work with Emily, why we collect it, who else sees it, and what your rights are.

We've written it in plain English because you deserve to understand it, not just be told it exists.

Who we are

This website is operated by Emily Witt, trading as Space Between Wellness (and Emily Witt Consulting for B2B engagements). Emily is an Australian sole trader based in Hobart, Tasmania.

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, you can reach Emily directly at space.between.wellness@gmail.com. That's a real inbox Emily checks. You'll get a real reply.

What information we collect

When you visit this website

Like almost every website, this one collects some information automatically when you arrive. Through Google Analytics 4 (GA4), we see things like:

  • Your approximate location (city/region level, not your street address)
  • The device and browser you're using
  • Which pages you visited and roughly how long you spent on them
  • How you found us - search engine, social media, a direct link
  • Your IP address (anonymised before it reaches us)

We also use the Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram's tracking tool), which places a small piece of code on this site. It records certain events - like viewing a page or clicking the application button - and sends them to Meta. This is what allows us to run advertising on Facebook and Instagram, and to show ads to people who are similar to those who've already visited or engaged. More on that in the cookies section.

Google Search Console is also connected to this site - that tool only shows us how the site appears in Google search results, and doesn't collect any personal information about you.

When you fill in the application form

The application form asks for:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • Your general location
  • Your age range
  • Free-text answers about where you are right now - your nervous system, your patterns, what's brought you here

Those free-text answers often include personal and emotional information. That's intentional - Emily asks those questions because she wants to understand where you actually are, not a surface-level summary of it. She reads every application herself.

Note on the form processor: Application submissions are handled by a third-party form tool (currently to be confirmed - either Formspree or GHL/HighLevel). This policy will be updated once that's finalised. Either way, your submission goes directly to Emily.

When you sign up for emails

If you opt in to receive emails from Emily, we collect your name and email address. These are stored in Flodesk, which is the email platform we use. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email.

When you book a session

Booking functionality is currently being finalised (likely via GHL or Cal.com). If you book through the site, we'll collect whatever information is needed to confirm and manage your appointment. This policy will be updated with specifics once that's live.

Why we collect it

Here's the short version:

  • Application form responses - so Emily can read your situation, respond personally, and understand whether her work is the right fit for where you are. She doesn't use a form assistant or pre-screening tool. It's her, reading it.
  • Email sign-up - so she can send you the content you've asked to receive. That's it.
  • Analytics data - so she can understand how people find the site and which parts are actually useful, and make it better over time.
  • Meta Pixel events - to help run advertising on Facebook and Instagram. This includes showing ads to people who've visited the site (retargeting) and to new audiences who look similar to existing visitors (lookalike audiences).

We don't collect information to build profiles, sell anything to third parties, or hand you off to automated systems. Emily's practice is small and intentional, and she treats the information she holds with the same care she brings to her client work.

Sensitive information - a specific note

The application form asks about your nervous system, your emotional patterns, and your personal experience. Under Australian privacy law, information about a person's psychological or emotional state may qualify as sensitive information and is held to a higher standard of care.

Emily takes that seriously. Here's what that looks like in practice:

  • She reads applications herself and does not share them with anyone else
  • Application responses are stored securely and are not used for marketing purposes
  • If you want your application deleted at any point - whether you're in an active process or not - you can email space.between.wellness@gmail.com and it will be done
  • This is coaching intake information, not clinical data - see the scope of practice section below for more on that distinction

Who else sees your data

We work with a small number of third-party tools to run this site. Here's who they are and what they see:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) - receives page view data and custom events (such as "viewed the apply page") via the Meta Pixel. Meta uses this to power ad retargeting. Meta has its own privacy policy: facebook.com/policy.
  • Google - receives anonymised analytics data via GA4 and site performance data via Search Console. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Flodesk - holds email subscriber names and addresses for the purpose of sending newsletters and updates. Flodesk is a US-based platform and handles data in accordance with its own privacy policy: flodesk.com/privacy. This is our current platform; this policy will note any changes.
  • Form processor (TBD) - application form submissions are processed by a third-party tool (Formspree or GHL - to be confirmed). This policy will be updated with the specific provider and their privacy terms once finalised.
  • Booking platform (TBD) - booking data will be held by our booking tool once that's live (likely GHL or Cal.com). This policy will be updated once confirmed.
  • GitHub Pages - this website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which is infrastructure provided by GitHub Inc. (a US-based company). GitHub hosts the site files. It does not have access to your form submissions or email data. GitHub's privacy statement: github.com/privacy.

Emily does not sell your data. To anyone. Ever. That's not a legal boilerplate line - it's just a fact about how this practice operates.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device. They're used to remember things between visits and to enable tracking tools to work.

This site uses cookies for two main purposes:

  • Analytics - Google Analytics 4 places cookies to track sessions and understand how the site is being used
  • Advertising - The Meta Pixel places cookies that allow Facebook and Instagram to recognise your browser if you visit those platforms later, enabling retargeting

You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. The exact steps depend on which browser you use - most have a "Privacy" or "Security" section in their settings where you can manage this. If you disable cookies, the site will still work fine, and disabling them won't prevent your form submission from reaching Emily. It will, however, affect how accurately the analytics and advertising tracking function.

You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking specifically by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. For Meta ad preferences, you can manage those via facebook.com/ads/preferences.

Your rights

If you're in Australia

Under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Request access to that information
  • Ask us to correct information that's wrong or out of date
  • Ask us to delete your information (subject to any legal obligations we have to keep it)
  • Make a complaint if you think your privacy rights have been breached

For any of the above, email space.between.wellness@gmail.com. Emily will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

If you're not satisfied with how a complaint is handled, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.

If you're in the EU or UK

This site is operated in Australia and does not actively target EU or UK residents. That said, if you're in the EU or UK and you do use this site, you may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR, including the right to access, correct, erase, or object to how your data is processed. Please email space.between.wellness@gmail.com and we'll work through your request. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you're in California (US)

This site does not sell personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). If you're a California resident and have questions about your data, reach out to space.between.wellness@gmail.com.

How long we keep your information

  • Application responses - kept while you're in an active coaching conversation or consideration process, and then for up to 24 months of no contact (in case you come back). You can request deletion at any time and we'll act on it promptly.
  • Email subscriber data - held for as long as you remain subscribed. When you unsubscribe, your details are removed from the active list. You can also request complete deletion by emailing us directly.
  • Analytics data - held by Google in accordance with GA4's data retention settings (typically 14 months by default).
  • Session booking data - to be confirmed once booking infrastructure is finalised.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold - using secure platforms, not storing sensitive information in places it doesn't need to be, and limiting who has access to what. Emily's is a small practice, which actually helps here: fewer hands means fewer points of risk.

That said, no internet-based system is 100% secure. We can't guarantee that information transmitted to or from this site is completely protected from interception or breach. If you have specific concerns about sharing sensitive information, you're always welcome to reach out directly by email first.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated as the tools and integrations on this site are confirmed and go live - particularly around the form processor and booking platform. We'll note the date of each update at the top of this page.

If we make material changes - the kind that meaningfully affect how your information is used - and you're subscribed to Emily's emails, we'll let you know via email.

About the scope of Emily's work

Emily Witt holds a degree in psychology and brings that background into her coaching and wellbeing education practice. She has worked for eight years in complex trauma settings. Her work is evidence-informed and draws on nervous system science, psychological frameworks, and spiritual wisdom.

Emily is not a registered psychologist, psychiatrist, or counsellor. The coaching and education she provides through Space Between Wellness is not a substitute for clinical mental health treatment, therapy, or medical advice. If what you're experiencing requires clinical support, please seek it.
