Emily Witt
Sound familiar?

Successful on the outside. Stuck in the same patterns on the inside.

  • You rewrite the email four times, then replay the meeting on the drive home

  • You hit the goal you set, and before it has even landed you have moved the finish line

  • You say yes with a full calendar, then quietly resent everyone who asked

  • You delegate the task, then check it, then end up redoing it yourself

  • You are at dinner with the people you love and your mind is still at work

  • You know you need to set the boundary, make the decision or take the opportunity. When the moment comes, you do what you always do

Yes, This Is Me
The real cost

And it is costing you more than time.

  • The opportunity you keep letting pass because, deep down, you doubt you have the capacity for more when you are already struggling at the level you are at
  • The hours spent second-guessing a decision you are more than qualified to make, then asking three people what they think anyway
  • The evenings you cannot switch off, and the fatigue that still lands after the holiday
  • The milestone you worked so hard for that felt good for about a day before you were onto the next one

You often know exactly what you should do. The way you respond in the moments that matter keeps pulling you back into familiar patterns. None of this means something is wrong with you. Most of these patterns were once smart ways to cope. They may just not be built for the next chapter you are creating.

Looking underneath

What you think you need may not be the whole story.

What you think you need

"I just need to be more disciplined."

What might actually be happening

You are overcommitting because saying no feels uncomfortable, and measuring your worth by how much you get done.

How coaching helps

We identify the pattern, understand what keeps it going, and practise a different response in the moments it usually takes over.

Every woman is different, so this is something we explore together rather than assume.

Meet Emily

Understanding why we do what we do is one thing. Learning how to change it is another.

I'm Emily, the founder of Space Between Wellness. I have a Bachelor of Psychological Science and almost a decade of experience working with people navigating complex trauma, adversity and challenging life circumstances.

That work has given me a deep understanding of how our experiences, beliefs and learned patterns shape the way we respond to ourselves, to other people and to the pressure we live under. How worth gets tied to achievement. How being capable becomes being responsible for everything. How the strategies that once kept us safe quietly start running our lives.

I also know this gap personally. I did everything the world said would make me feel successful, and the biggest shift came when I started asking what was actually true for me.

Understanding a pattern is where change starts, and it rarely finishes there. My coaching is psychology-informed, trauma-informed and strengths-based, bringing together self-understanding and practical behaviour change. The focus is on self-trust, identity and doing something different in your everyday life, so what you know about yourself finally shows up in how you live.

  • Bachelor of Psychological Science

  • Trained in crisis intervention and family support, with ongoing professional learning

My coaching is psychology-informed and non-clinical. I am not a registered psychologist or counsellor, and I do not diagnose or treat mental health conditions.

How I work · The Space Between Method

The bridge between knowing and becoming.

Where you areOverthinking, overextending, second-guessing yourself, repeating familiar behaviours and struggling to follow through on changes you know you want.

Stage 1 One

Recognise

Understand what's keeping you stuck.

Explore the beliefs, recurring patterns, learned expectations and automatic responses shaping how you think, feel and act.

Stage 2 Two

Restore

Build the capacity to respond differently.

Practical skills for noticing stress, creating space before an automatic reaction, understanding your capacity and meeting hard moments with more flexibility.

Stage 3 Three

Realign

Reconnect with who you want to become.

Explore your values, needs, desires and direction. Question the expectations you have outgrown and build a more intentional relationship with yourself and your goals.

Stage 4 Four

Reclaim

Turn awareness into lived change.

Practise new behaviours and decisions in real situations, and build self-trust through follow-through and evidence that you can respond differently.

Where you're working towardGreater self-trust, clearer decisions, healthier boundaries, more capacity and intentional action, and the ability to pursue success while actually being present in your own life.

The four Rs are the map for the whole work, and moving through them takes time and practice. Your Deep Dive focuses on the pattern that matters most right now and gives you a personalised action plan for your next steps.
