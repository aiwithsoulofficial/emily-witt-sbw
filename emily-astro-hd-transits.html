What Being a Projector Means

Projectors make up about 20% of the population. You are not here to hustle, grind, or push content into the void hoping someone notices. You are here to be recognised for your wisdom and invited into spaces where your guidance can land. This is a fundamental shift from how most business coaches teach visibility.

Your strategy - wait for the invitation - does not mean sit in a corner and hope someone finds you. It means build something so clearly aligned with your depth and expertise that the right people come to you. Your website, your content, your frameworks - these are your invitations. When someone lands on your work and feels that "yes" in their body, they are inviting themselves into your field.

As a Projector, you are not designed for sustained energy output the way Generators and Manifestors are.
