## What NOT to put in the bio

- No emojis (project rule + brand voice)
- No emdashes — middle dots (·) are fine, pipes (|) are corporate
- No "transforming lives" / "empowering women" generic wellness language
- No certifications / credentials beyond "psych grad" - those land in the about section
- No price points or session-length specs in bio
- No "DM for inquiries" - the bio link should do that work
