01 — Brand Essence What we stand for

Who Emily is. Who this is for. Why it exists.

"I want to stop women from building these empires that they are miserable in - fueled by feeling unworthy, fueled by expectations."
Emily Witt

Space Between Wellness is a nervous system coaching and wellbeing education practice founded by Emily Witt in Hobart, Tasmania. Emily works with high-achieving women who built the life they were told to want and somehow lost themselves along the way - women who are exhausted in a way that sleep doesn't fix, who have a spiritual side they've been told isn't professional, and who know something needs to change even when everything looks fine from the outside.

The work is grounded in psychological science - eight years working in trauma and complex trauma in the community sector, a Bachelor of Psychological Science, and training in trauma-informed practice. And it is woven openly with genuine spiritual wisdom. Not as decoration. As the point. Emily stopped studying further psychology because it kept excluding the parts that actually mattered - the intuitive, the embodied, the parts of human experience that science alone can't hold. So she built something that bridges both.

Space Between Wellness exists because high-capacity women deserve a container that holds all of them - the capable and the worn out, the spiritual and the evidence-driven, the version performing okayness and the one who's fed up. Emily's signature phrase - "evidence-informed guidance woven with spiritual wisdom" - is the truest description of the brand. The nervous system is the entry point. Coming home to yourself is the destination.

The pathway back to you
Who she serves

High-achieving women - navigating burnout, identity shifts, life transitions, or the quiet sense that they've done everything right and it still doesn't feel like enough.

What she offers

1:1 breakthrough sessions, in-person sound baths, an online community container, and organisational wellbeing education for purpose-driven teams. All through the Space Between Method.

The differentiator

Spirituality is not a bolt-on. It is the differentiator. This is psychological science and spiritual wisdom together - for the whole person, not the performing version of her.

02 — Voice & Tone How Emily sounds

Locked May 2026

Emily's voice is her voice - not a brand filter. Warm and professional in the same breath. Spirituality woven openly. Honest enough to name what nobody else will. Every piece of copy should sound like Emily talking, not like a wellness brand.

Warm Professional Spiritual Evidence-informed Honest Direct Supportive Challenging
Do say
DO "You're not burned out. You're just... fed up." - names the real thing without clinical distancing
DO "Your fire didn't go out. It's buried under years of doing everything for everyone else." - honest, emotionally true, no gloss
DO "Evidence-informed guidance woven with spiritual wisdom." - names both pillars with equal weight
DO "Build from desire, not from wound." - poetic but grounded, distinctly Emily
Don't say
DON'T "Burnout is an epidemic affecting high performers..." - stat-hook, clinical distance, generic opener
DON'T "This isn't therapy - it's coaching." - the "not X, it's Y" contrast pattern. Just say what it IS.
DON'T "90% of high-achieving women report..." - tech/stat hooks are an AI tell and strip intimacy
DON'T "Unlock your potential and transform your mindset." - corporate wellness language. No.
Locked copy rules
"Fed up" not "burnout" - Emily's clients are fed up with performing a version of their life. "Burnout" is overused and clinically flattened. Use "fed up", "exhausted", "quietly relentless", "done."
Nervous system states have names - Activation, Over Giving, Shut Down, Regulated. Never use fight/flight/fawn/freeze in client-facing copy. Those are clinical terms Emily has translated into language that lands.
Spirituality is the differentiator - weave it openly - never footnote or apologise for the spiritual dimension. It is equal footing with the science. Clients who want both have been waiting for this.
The signature line is locked - "Evidence-informed guidance woven with spiritual wisdom." Use it as written, not paraphrased.
No emdashes in copy - use a hyphen with spaces ( - ) or rewrite. Emdashes read as corporate.
No "not X, it's Y" contrast pattern - it is an AI tell and weakens the message. State what it IS with confidence.
No stat or tech hooks - never lead with data points or counts. Lead with the emotional truth of what the client is living in right now.
Voice = Emily talking - every piece of copy should pass the test: does this sound like Emily speaking to a woman she genuinely cares about? Corporate framing, wellness cliches, and motivational language all fail this test.

05 — The Method Four stages

The Space Between Method

A four-stage framework rooted in nervous system science, psychological research, and the wisdom of your own lived experience. The stages always flow in order: Recognise leads to Restore leads to Realign leads to Reclaim.

Stage 1 One

Recognise

Name what's actually happening beneath the surface. Identify the patterns, the nervous system responses, the beliefs that have been running the show.

→
Stage 2 Two

Restore

Create safety in the body before trying to change anything. Rest, regulation, and rebuilding the foundations that support sustainable wellbeing.

→
Stage 3 Three

Realign

Reconnect with your actual values, not the ones you inherited or performed. Clarify what success means to you on your own terms.

→
Stage 4 Four

Reclaim

Step back into your life with intention. Build from desire, not from wound. Move forward in a way that actually feels like you.

Recognise · Restore · Realign · Reclaim
06 — Nervous System States Naming what's real

Activation / Over Giving / Shut Down / Regulated

These are Emily's translations of clinical nervous system terminology into language that actually lands with the women she works with. These four terms are brand-locked. Never use fight/flight/fawn/freeze in client-facing copy.

Activation

Fight response

Driven, restless, reactive. Running on adrenaline and mistaking it for motivation. Everything feels urgent. Rest feels impossible.

Over Giving

Fawn response

People-pleasing, saying yes when your body is screaming no. Made yourself so agreeable you've lost track of what you actually want.

Shut Down

Freeze response

Numb, foggy, disconnected. The emotional dial turned down so low you feel like you're watching your life from behind glass.

Regulated

Safety response

Present, grounded, clear. Feel the full range of emotions without being controlled by them. This is where growth, connection, and creativity live.

Most high-achieving women cycle between three of these. Very few are living in the fourth. The clinical terms (fight/flight/fawn/freeze) appear only in the smaller sub-labels on cards - never as the primary terminology in copy, emails, or social content.

Logo design brief notes

When a logo is designed, it should work in two forms: wordmark only (Playfair italic "Space Between Wellness") and a combined mark with a minimal icon or monogram. The icon should reference the concept of "between" or "threshold" - not a leaf, lotus, or wellness cliche. Think negative space, a gap, stillness between two elements.

Colour: primary in --earth on light, --blush-light on dark. Lockup options: horizontal, stacked, icon-only for social profile. Must work at 32px (favicon) up to full-width print.
