Email 2 - Day 2

Subject Lines

A. (direct) The thing I want you to know before anything else. B. (contemplative) You're not broken. Your nervous system is running someone else's programme. C. (curious) What if the problem isn't you?

Preview Text

There's a difference between broken and patterned.

Hi [first_name],

Can I share something that took me a long time to actually understand?

You're not broken. You're patterned.

There's a huge difference. Broken implies something went wrong with you. Patterned means your nervous system learned exactly what it needed to learn to keep you safe - and now it's running that same programme on a loop, even when the original threat is long gone.

That programme might look like relentless doing when you'd rather slow down. Or saying yes when your whole body is screaming no. Or going numb. Watching your life from a slight distance and wondering when you'll actually feel present in it.

In my work, I use four states to describe where your nervous system is living on any given day. You'll hear me talk about these a lot.

Activation - driven, restless, always on. Rest feels impossible.

Over Giving - agreeable, giving, endlessly accommodating. You've lost track of what you actually want.

Shut Down - foggy, flat, disconnected. Going through the motions.

Regulated - present, grounded, clear. This is where the good stuff lives.

Most of the women I work with are cycling between the first three. Very few are regularly in the fourth. And the wild thing is, most of them didn't know they had a name for what they were feeling.

If you want to know which state you've been living in, I made a short quiz for exactly this.

Take the quiz - What's really going on?

It takes two minutes and it might give you the clearest language you've had for this in a while.

Em x

Email 3 - Day 5

Subject Lines

Preview Text

I stopped studying further psychology because it kept leaving out the parts that mattered.

Hi [first_name],

I want to tell you a bit about why I do this work the way I do.

My background is in psychological science. I spent eight years in the community sector working with trauma and complex trauma. I've sat with a lot of people in some of their hardest moments. I know what the research says. I trust it.

And I stopped pursuing further formal psychology training because it kept asking me to leave certain things at the door.

The intuitive parts. The spiritual parts. The parts of human experience that neuroscience can describe but can't fully hold. I got tired of pretending those didn't exist just to be taken seriously.

So I built something that doesn't ask you to choose.

The Space Between Method is grounded in nervous system science, trauma-informed practice, and psychological research. It's also woven with genuine spiritual wisdom - not the performative kind. The kind that actually moves something in you.

One of my clients wrote this after a session:

"It was like something drew me toward you, knowing I was in for a bad day. It was truly something special - like a reset and rest my body truly needed."

That's the thing about this work. It's not something I do to you. It's something we find together, when you feel safe enough to stop performing and actually arrive.

If you've got a spiritual side you've been hiding because someone told you it wasn't professional - this is a space where you don't have to hide it anymore.

I'd love to hear where you are right now. Not in a formal way, just - if you feel like replying, tell me which nervous system state feels most familiar. Activation, Over Giving, Shut Down, or Regulated. No obligation. I read every reply.
