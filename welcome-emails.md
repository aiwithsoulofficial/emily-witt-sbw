## Email 2 - Day 2

### Subject Lines

A. (direct) The thing I want you to know before anything else.
B. (contemplative) You're not broken. Your nervous system is running someone else's programme.
C. (curious) What if the problem isn't you?

### Preview Text
There's a difference between broken and patterned.

---

Hi [first_name],

Can I share something that took me a long time to actually understand?

You're not broken. You're patterned.

There's a huge difference. Broken implies something went wrong with you. Patterned means your nervous system learned exactly what it needed to learn to keep you safe - and now it's running that same programme on a loop, even when the original threat is long gone.

That programme might look like relentless doing when you'd rather slow down. Or saying yes when your whole body is screaming no. Or going numb. Watching your life from a slight distance and wondering when you'll actually feel present in it.

In my work, I use four states to describe where your nervous system is living on any given day. You'll hear me talk about these a lot.

Activation - driven, restless, always on. Rest feels impossible.

Over Giving - agreeable, giving, endlessly accommodating. You've lost track of what you actually want.

Shut Down - foggy, flat, disconnected. Going through the motions.

Regulated - present, grounded, clear. This is where the good stuff lives.

Most of the women I work with are cycling between the first three. Very few are regularly in the fourth. And the wild thing is, most of them didn't know they had a name for what they were feeling.

If you want to know which state you've been living in, I made a short quiz for exactly this.

[Take the quiz - What's really going on?](https://aiwithsoulofficial.github.io/emily-witt-sbw/#nervous-system)

It takes two minutes and it might give you the clearest language you've had for this in a while.

Em x
