The cost

And it is costing you more than time.

  • The opportunity you keep letting pass because, deep down, you doubt you have the capacity for more when you are already struggling at the level you are at
  • The hours spent second-guessing a decision you are more than qualified to make, then asking three people what they think anyway
  • The evenings you cannot switch off, and the fatigue that still lands after the holiday
  • The milestone you worked so hard for that felt good for about a day before you were onto the next one
What I've noticed

Awareness isn't always what's missing. You can understand exactly why you do something and still struggle to respond differently when it matters.

You have probably done the reading and the podcasts, maybe years of therapy. The insight is there. What is harder is catching the pattern in the moment and doing something different with it. That is what this session is for.

What happens in the session

Three parts. One clear plan.

  1. Uncover the patterns keeping you stuck

    Where you keep getting caught: overthinking a decision, saying yes when you mean no, not letting go of the work, moving the finish line. Rather than handing you a label, I ask questions that help you see what the pattern means to you and what it might be protecting. We also name the strengths you can lean on.

  2. Look at how stress is showing up for you

    Using the four SBW patterns (Activation, Over Giving, Shut Down and Regulated) as a reflective guide, we get clear on where you spend most of your time and what your capacity really is, so the plan fits your actual life.

  3. Leave with your personalised action plan

    Next steps for your priority pattern, mapped to the four Rs. Tools you can use straight away, and a clear first step to put into practice this week.

After our session you get your own private Notion portal with your key patterns, strengths, stress-response pattern, action steps, the tools we covered and an implementation dashboard to track how it goes.
Is this for you?

This is for you if

  • You are capable and doing well on paper
  • You can name your patterns and still keep living them
  • You overthink, over-function or find it hard to switch off
  • You want something specific to work with, beyond more insight

This is not the right fit if

  • You are in crisis or need clinical mental health support
  • You are looking for a diagnosis or treatment
  • You want someone to fix it for you without trying anything new

Questions
Can one session really help?

One session will not undo years of habit, and I won't promise that it will. What it can give you is a clear understanding of the pattern that matters most right now, where your nervous system is at, and a realistic place to start. If you want support to keep practising over time, The Bridge, my six-week private intensive, is coming soon.

Is this therapy?

No. This is coaching. It is educational and non-clinical, and I do not diagnose, assess or treat mental health conditions. If you need clinical support, I will always encourage you to see a registered psychologist, GP or counsellor. Coaching can sit alongside that work.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Everything shared is handled with care and confidentiality, in line with professional ethical standards. The only exceptions are legal obligations, such as a risk of harm to yourself or others. This is explained before we begin.

Can I claim Medicare or private health rebates?

No. Sessions are coaching and education, which is not eligible for rebates.
